Master laser-guided shots, silence DJ mosquitoes, and guard Uncle's sleep!

Controls (Desktop & Android)

  • Desktop Mouse: Hover anywhere to lock the dotted laser sight; Left Click fires instantly.
  • Desktop Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ to aim, Space to blast, R to tactical reload, P/Esc pause, M mute, F fullscreen.
  • Android / Touch: Drag anywhere to sweep your laser sight cleanly, then release to fire. Tap the ammo pill anytime to reload early.

Mosquito Squad

  • Buzzer (100 pts): 1 HP standard intruder.
  • DJ Singer (300 pts): Plays bedroom beats — blast mid-song for an instant MIC DROP!
  • Shadow Ninja (150 pts): Fast flyer that dodges laser locks.
  • Armored Tank (250 pts): 2 HP helmeted tank; first hit stuns with a BONK!
  • King Mosquito (1,000 pts): 6 HP Boss on Boss Nights.

Power-Ups & Fever Streaks

  • Zap Ammo: Unlimited rapid shots + electric chain blast (2 damage).
  • Sleep Coil: Slows every mosquito in the room by 52%.
  • Deep Sleep: Restores +1 Sleep Heart.
  • 5x Fever Streak: Every 5 combo kills awards +5 Sleep Coins automatically.

Trophies & Fair Stamina

  • 6 Career Trophies: First Swat, Fever Hands (8x combo), Mic Dropper, Sniper Uncle (85%+ accuracy), King Slayer, and Night Watchman (25+ Endless). Each awards +10 Coins.
  • Fair Stamina: Winning Campaign Nights costs 0 stamina. Only failed nights cost 1 bolt (auto-regenerates every 10 minutes), and Endless Night is always free.
