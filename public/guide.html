Machchhar: Raat ka Daaku — Field Manual

Master laser-guided shots, silence DJ mosquitoes, and protect Uncle's sleep!

▶ PLAY NOW

🎮 Controls (Desktop & Mobile)

  • Desktop Mouse: Hover anywhere to lock the dotted laser sight; Left Click shoots instantly!
  • Desktop Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ to aim, Space to blast, R to tactical reload, P/Esc pause, M mute, F fullscreen.
  • Android / Touch: Drag anywhere to aim the laser sight cleanly, then release to fire! Tap the ammo pill anytime to reload early.

🦟 Mosquito Field Guide

  • 🦟 Buzzer (100 pts): 1 HP standard intruder.
  • 🎧 DJ Singer (300 pts): Plays catchy bedroom beats—blast mid-song for an instant MIC DROP!
  • 🥷 Shadow Ninja (150 pts): Fast flyer that can juke your laser sight!
  • 🛡️ Armored Tank (250 pts): 2 HP helmeted tank; first hit stuns with a BONK!
  • 👑 King Mosquito (1,000 pts): 6 HP Boss on Boss Nights!

⚡ Power-Ups & Streaks

  • ⚡ Zap Ammo: Unlimited rapid fire + chain blast (ignores reload & deals 2 damage).
  • 🌀 Sleep Coil: Slows every mosquito in the room by 52% for easy snipes.
  • 💖 Deep Sleep: Restores +1 Sleep Heart when Uncle gets bitten.
  • 🔥 Fever Streaks: Every 5 combo kills awards +5 Sleep Coins automatically!

🏆 Achievements & Sleep Stamina

  • 6 Unlockable Badges: First Swat, Fever Hands (8x combo), Mic Dropper, Sniper Uncle (85%+ accuracy), King Slayer, and Night Watchman (25+ in Endless). Each badge awards +10 🪙!
  • Fair Stamina: Winning Campaign Nights costs 0 stamina. Only failed attempts cost 1 bolt (auto-regenerates every 10 minutes), and Endless Night is always free.
