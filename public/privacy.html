Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · App: Machchhar: Raat ka Daaku (com.mct.machchhar)

This Privacy Policy explains how Machchhar: Raat ka Daaku ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you play our mobile or web game.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Machchhar: Raat ka Daaku does not require account registration, login, or submission of your name, phone number, or address. Your high scores, unlocked levels, stars, virtual coins, stamina, and audio settings are stored locally on your device using browser/WebView local storage.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob / AdSense)

To keep the game free to play, Machchhar: Raat ka Daaku displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob (on Android) or Google Ads (on Web). Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device information, including:

  • Advertising ID (AAID) or similar device identifiers
  • IP address (used for approximate country/region estimation and fraud prevention)
  • App interaction, ad impression, and crash/diagnostic metrics

For more details, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising on Android via Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. Children's Privacy

Machchhar: Raat ka Daaku is a general-audience arcade game and is not knowingly directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service, please contact us so we can assist.

5. Data Deletion

Because game save data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all local game progress anytime by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App. See our Support & Data Deletion page for details.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
