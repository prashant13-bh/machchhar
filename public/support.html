How to Delete Your Game Data

Machchhar: Raat ka Daaku does not create user accounts or store personal profiles on developer servers. All game scores, stars, coins, and stamina are stored strictly on your device:

  • Android App: Open Settings → Apps → Machchhar: Raat ka Daaku → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data, or uninstall the app.
  • Web Browser: Clear site data / localStorage for the game domain in your browser settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why didn't sound play at first?
Tap anywhere on the screen or click Play to unlock device audio, and check that the top-right speaker icon is set to 🔊.
How does Sleep Stamina work?
Winning a night is free (0 stamina cost). Losing costs 1 stamina bolt, which regenerates automatically every 10 minutes—or you can refill instantly via coins or an optional rewarded ad, or play Endless Night anytime for free.
